Sunday lunch

Long lunches, from twelve.

Sunday is its own thing here — we open the dining room from noon and run through the afternoon. Roasts go out steadily, puddings follow, and the room takes on the unhurried pace of a Sunday well spent.

Sunday roast
Beef, lamb or pork. Yorkshire puddings. Cauliflower cheese. Roast potatoes done properly. Gravy.

Outside catering

We will cook for your occasion.

We've cooked for the kinds of days you tend to remember: birthdays in marquees, christenings at home, office gatherings, wakes that need feeding kindly. The kitchen travels well, and we approach it with the same care we give the restaurant itself.

Tell us roughly what the day looks like — how many, when, where, what mood — and we'll come back with a sensible suggestion and a clear cost. We don't over-promise on numbers we can't do well; if we say yes, we mean it.
