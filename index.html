A short story

A little restaurant
on a quiet street, with proper cooking.

Grants sits tucked along Norfolk Street, a short walk from the Tuesday Market Place. The dining room is small. The welcome is warm. The food is the kind that doesn't need explaining: a prawn cocktail set well, a beef and ale pie under a proper crust, a banoffee pie made the way it used to be.

It is family-run — Anna, Kim and Les look after the room and the kitchen — and that is what most regulars come back for. The cooking, then the company.
