Getting here

A two-minute walk from the Tuesday Market Place.

Norfolk Street runs north-east out of the old town. We sit roughly halfway along, on a quiet stretch of independent shopfronts.

The closest car parks are Austin Street and the St James multi-storey, both a short walk away. King's Lynn railway station is around ten minutes on foot.

If you have not been before — look for the small frontage, the warm light in the window, and the painted lettering on the door.

Pick up the phone. We'll see you Friday.
